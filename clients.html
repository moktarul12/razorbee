CapItCap — capital, made approachable

Fintech Crowdfunding Platform engineering Investor UX

CapItCap is a boutique equity crowdfunding destination — businesses raise, investors participate. Razorbee delivered the software backbone: onboarding, campaign surfaces, and investment journeys that stay precise as complexity scales.

The brief was clarity under pressure. We designed flows for issuers launching offerings and investors evaluating opportunities beyond traditional exchanges.

  • Investor & issuer onboarding with verification structure
  • Campaign pages for equity, debt, and convertibles
  • Investment checkout / participation experience
  • Data structures for escrow, cap-table, and compliance docs
