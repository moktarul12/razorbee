The overlap is the office.

Global is not a footer list of cities. It is a designed overlap: who decides, when, and what is written so the rest of the day can proceed.

Write the decision down

A call across twelve hours is expensive. We use it for judgment, not for status. Status lives in the ticket, the preview, the changelog. If a Sydney partner wakes up to a question that could have been a paragraph, we failed the overlap.

The map is concrete: Kolkata, Bangalore, Sydney, Singapore, Dubai, London, New York. Registered office in Kolkata, workshop in Bangalore, partnerships everywhere else.

Follow the sun without losing the plot

Australia gets a morning review of what India built. Gulf and Europe sit in the middle of the day. New York closes loops that should not wait another rotation. The method keeps one backlog so the sun does not fork the product into seven versions.

This is also why deploys are small. A large Friday surprise is a cruelty to whoever is on the other side of the date line.
